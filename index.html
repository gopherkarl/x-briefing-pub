"The biggest concern I think I have is it seems to suggest "rationality" is identical to something like "logic" or a purely deductive process. But the whole Popperian view is that rationality *is* creativity: the *creation* of corrections to errors once identified, etc."

🔑 Key Takeaway

Rationality is not deduction; it is the creative act of generating new conjectures that correct identified errors. Logic can only check arguments, never originate them—so reducing reason to a deductive algorithm quietly eliminates the very thing that makes the growth of knowledge possible: the imagination that proposes a better explanation where none existed.
