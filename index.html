A sharp statement of the fallibilist distinction between a subject matter and our knowledge of it. Keeping the two apart is the precondition for taking criticism and progress seriously.

We explore the architectural progression from local parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) on Apple Silicon to an agile, agentic "thin-client" system powered by frontier models. We contrast the limitations of small-scale open-weights model fine-tuning against structured context engineering and tool-driven learning, framing the entire engineering loop through Karl Popper’s critical rationalism. Finally, we detail the real-world constraints, diagnostic protocols, and security roadblocks of integrating automated agents into the sandboxed WhatsApp ecosystem, culminating in the detection and mitigation of a silent session restriction (shadow-ban).
