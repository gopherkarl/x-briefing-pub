This paper documents the 48-hour rapid development and deployment of "Sandeep Bot," an automated conversational agent engineered to replicate the highly nuanced, bilingual (Hinglish/Urdu) comedic style of a specific WhatsApp group participant. We explore the architectural progression from local parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) on Apple Silicon to an agile, agentic "thin-client" system powered by frontier models. Finally, we detail the real-world constraints, diagnostic protocols, and security roadblocks of integrating automated agents into the sandboxed WhatsApp ecosystem, culminating in the detection and mitigation of a silent session restriction (shadow-ban).
