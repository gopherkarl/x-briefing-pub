🔑 Key Takeaway

A precise distinction between mechanical proof and explanation. ToKTeacher separates the formal, rule-bound derivation of a conclusion from the understanding of what it means - the first is algorithmic symbol-manipulation, the second an explanation of why the conclusion is true and what it reveals about the world. This is the core of critical rationalism: knowledge worth having is explanatory, not merely derivational. The remark guards against the temptation to treat a machine-checked result as if it were understanding, and it carries directly into the AGI debate - because computation (proving) is not the same thing as explaining, an agent that can automate proofs has not thereby acquired understanding.
