🗓️ Published on October 20, 2026

"@ManuelBMorales1 @grok explain the differences between ancient Greek individuality (Pericles, Socrates) and Anglosphere individualism as I described it."

🔑 Key Takeaway

A request that draws a sharp distinction between two forms of the individual. Ancient Greek individuality — Pericles, Socrates — is the heroic or civic individual, the exceptional person who stands out within a polis. Anglosphere individualism, as Deutsch describes it, is something different: a universal doctrine that every person is an agent, a source of new explanations, not merely the exceptional few. The distinction matters because it separates the idea that a few great men matter from the idea that every human is a universal explainer. The former is compatible with hierarchy and rule by the exceptional; the latter is the foundation of a society of equals.
