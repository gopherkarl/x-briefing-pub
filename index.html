🔑 Key Takeaway

Falsification is only as strong as a claim's willingness to be abandoned when it fails. Recalibrating a prior after the predicted date passes without consequence is not learning but immunisation: it converts refutation into confirmation and removes the very outcome that could have counted against the theory. A conjecture that cannot lose is not knowledge.
